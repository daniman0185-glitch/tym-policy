Privacy Policy — Test Your Memory

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy describes how Test Your Memory (“the app”) handles information.

1. Information we collect

  • Game data: your best results (stars, fewest moves) are stored only on your device. They never leave your device and we have no access to them.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob, our advertising partner, may collect your Advertising ID and technical device information to show ads.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, emails or passwords
  • No personal data
  • No location data

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. Google may use the Advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, subject to Google’s own privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Children

The game is family friendly and contains no violent or adult content.

5. Data deletion

All game data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything, including your records.
